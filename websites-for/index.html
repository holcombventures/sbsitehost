One service, tailored to each trade

SB Site Host builds, hosts and maintains websites for local service businesses for one monthly price. The core is the same for everyone — a fast, mobile-friendly site optimized for Google and AI search, with hosting, security, updates and monthly edits included. What changes is the content and layout: a plumber needs a giant call button, a roofer needs a project gallery and inspection form, and a massage therapist needs a calm service menu and booking link.

Each page below explains what customers in that industry look for, what we build, and which plan tends to fit. Each one also links to a live template preview using a fictional business so you can see the starting design.
