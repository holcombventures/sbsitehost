What a plumbing website actually needs

Plumbing is one of the most urgent searches a homeowner ever makes. People rarely browse; they search "emergency plumber near me," glance at two or three results, and call. That means the things that matter most are simple: a phone number that is tappable at the top of every page, a clear statement of whether you handle after-hours calls, and a list of the towns you actually serve.

Beyond emergencies, plumbers also win planned work — water heater replacements, repiping, fixture upgrades, sewer line repair. Those customers compare a few companies before calling, so they look for upfront pricing language, licensing and insurance details, warranty terms and real photos of your trucks and team.

What we build for your plumbing business

  • Click-to-call everywhere. A sticky call button on mobile, because most plumbing searches happen on a phone.
  • One page per core service. Drain cleaning, water heaters, leak detection, sewer lines and so on, each written so Google can match it to specific searches.
  • Service-area content. The cities and neighborhoods you cover, so you show up for local searches without stuffing keywords.
  • A short quote form. Name, phone, what's wrong. Long forms lose customers who are standing in water.
  • Google Business Profile setup so your map listing and your site tell the same story.

Showing up in AI answers

More homeowners now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI overviews "who's a good plumber in my town?" Those tools pull from sites with clear, structured information: business name, services, hours, service area and contact details marked up so machines can read them. Every SB Site Host plan includes that structured data, so your business is easier for AI tools to understand and cite.

Which plan fits a plumbing company?

A one- or two-truck shop that mostly needs to be found usually starts on Basic. If you want to capture leads after hours, Classic adds an AI chatbot that can answer common questions and collect job details, plus an instant quote tool for jobs like water heater swaps. Shops running ads or juggling several techs often choose VIP for call and lead tracking, so you can see which calls came from the website and what they turned into.
