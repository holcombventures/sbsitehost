Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SB Site Host, a brand of Holcombventures LLC ("we," "us"), collects and uses information when you visit sbsitehost.com or buy our services.

Information we collect

  • Contact information: name, email, phone and similar details you give us by email, at checkout, or during kickoff.
  • Business information: business name, address, services, pricing, logo, photos and other content you provide so we can build your site.
  • Payment information: handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number. We receive limited details from Stripe, such as your name, email, billing address, plan and payment status.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and font provider (Google Fonts) may automatically receive your IP address and browser details when you load our pages.
  • Analytics: we use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count visits in aggregate. GoatCounter does not set cookies and does not track you across sites. It records the page viewed, referring site, browser and operating system type, screen size, and approximate country (derived from your IP address, which is not stored). We also count clicks on our plan checkout buttons as anonymous events. We cannot identify individual visitors from this data. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on sbsitehost.com.

How we use it

  • To build, host, maintain and support your website
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To communicate with you about your account, service and policy changes
  • To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations

How we share it

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate, such as Stripe (payments, tax and billing portal), hosting providers, email providers and AI tools used to build your site, and when required by law or as part of a business transfer.

Customer websites

For sites we build for you, you are responsible for the privacy practices toward your own visitors. Features such as forms, chatbots, call tracking and analytics may collect visitor data on your behalf.

Retention

We keep information while your subscription is active and as needed afterward for legal, tax and accounting purposes. Hosted site content may be deleted 90 days after cancellation.

Your choices and rights
