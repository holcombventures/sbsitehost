FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is there really no upfront fee?

Right. Design and build are included in your monthly plan. You start paying when you subscribe and we start building.

How do the 14 days work?

After your kickoff call we deliver a live preview, refine it with your feedback, and launch by day 14 as long as we receive your content and feedback on time.

What counts as an "edit"?

Text, photo, price, hours and menu changes, new sections, blog posts. Just email or message us and we handle it within your plan's monthly hours.

Can I use my existing domain?

Yes. We connect your current domain or register a new one for you.

Can I change plans or add-ons later?

Anytime. Upgrade, downgrade or add power-ups from your dashboard and changes apply next billing cycle.

What happens if I cancel?

Your service runs through the end of the paid month. No cancellation fees.
